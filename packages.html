The Experience

Carnival Monday, start to finish.

One day, two vibes. Here's exactly what happens from the moment you arrive to the last note of Blackout.

LUX J'Ouvert · Sunrise

Assembly starts at 3:30 AM in San Fernando. By 4 AM, the music trucks roll and the road belongs to you — paint, powder, and premium drinks flowing until the sun comes up around 10 AM.

This isn't a free-for-all. Lux J'Ouvert is a premium experience: organized, secure, and intimate. Private security and roving medics walk the full route. Your waterproof phone case keeps your gear safe when the paint starts flying.

  • Assembly: 3:30 AM, San Fernando
  • Road out: 4:00 AM
  • Chip home: ~10:00 AM
  • Route pre-cleared with TTPS
  • Paint & powder provided — wear clothes you don't mind losing
  • Closed-toe footwear recommended

LUX Blackout · After Dark

Call-time 7:00 PM. Every masquerader lit up — LED glasses, fiber-optic wands, glow sticks, neon bunny ears. Monday Night Mas, done right. The brightest night on the road.

Blackout picks up where J'Ouvert leaves off. Rest during the day, then come back glowing. Neon pink or orange tees, full LED gear in the bag, unlimited drinks and music trucks rolling until late.

Can I do both?

Absolutely. They're two separate events on the same day — register for one or both. Many masqueraders go J'Ouvert in the morning, rest, then come back for Blackout after dark. Separate registration and separate bags for each.
Sizing & Costume Details

All apparel runs S / M / L / XL. The female J'Ouvert package includes a T-shirt plus two monokini styles (Style I and Style II) — each sized independently. A monokini is a one-piece styled swimsuit-cut costume piece. Colors: Black or White for J'Ouvert, Neon Pink or Neon Orange for Blackout.

No exchanges on collection day — packages are pre-made based on your selections. Take your time choosing sizes before submitting.
Payment & Refunds

Payment is collected in person by your assigned band rep — nothing to pay online. Installment plans available. Refunds: full up to 30 days before, 50% between 30–14 days, none within 14 days (but transferable to another masquerader).
